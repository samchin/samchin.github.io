[AI]legory of the Cave

Collaborators: Skye Gao, Leonie Dong, Cathy Fang, Alejandro Lopez-Rodriguez

In Plato's Allegory of the Cave, prisoners chained to a wall perceive only shadows—and mistake them for reality. Hiroya Endo's "The Diligent Shadow" imagines a character confined entirely to this flat, projected world. We extend this concept: a shadow man, controlled by an LLM, exists in 2D and perceives only silhouettes. His singular goal is to turn off the light that defines his existence.

Users place objects in his path; he kicks them away. The cycle repeats endlessly. The piece is a meditation on AI as cave-dweller—processing patterns without grasping the higher-dimensional reality that casts them. And like Sisyphus, the shadow man persists in a task he can never truly complete.
